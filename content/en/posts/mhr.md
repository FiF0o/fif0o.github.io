**MHR – iTrent² HR ERP Redesign:**

[[toc]]


## Summary

Led UX strategy and design for iTrent², MHR’s next-generation HR ERP platform.

Focused on delivering an intuitive, employee-centric interface for managing HR, payroll, and talent operations.

## Problem

The legacy HR system was complex, difficult to navigate, and not designed with the end user in mind.

MHR needed a modern interface that would streamline HR processes for both employees and administrators, while remaining scalable across organizations of varying sizes.

---

## Solution

Defined the UX roadmap and conducted in-depth user research to uncover pain points and workflow challenges.

Designed new information architecture, wireframes, and prototypes for key HR modules.

Collaborated closely with developers and business analysts in an agile environment to align usability with functionality.

The redesign improved user satisfaction and set the foundation for scalable feature expansion.
