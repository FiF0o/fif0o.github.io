**Renault – R-Link2 & Next-Gen Gear Stick:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Contributed to the UX design and HMI research for Renault’s R-Link2 system, focusing on the development of a next-generation automotive gear stick and its integration into the vehicle’s digital interface as well as Multimedia system.

## Problem

Renault aimed to modernize the driving experience by redesigning the gear stick to be more intuitive, aesthetically aligned with interior design, and better integrated with the vehicle’s HMI system.

The challenge was to meet high usability standards while coordinating with manufacturing and supplier constraints.

---

## Solution

Worked closely with product design, ergonomics, and HMI teams to define the user experience of the new gear selector and its interaction with the R-Link2 system.

Delivered detailed specifications, interface wireframes, and usability guidelines for suppliers. Supported testing sessions to validate behavior and ease of use.
 
The outcome was a sleek, driver-centered interface now used across Renault’s modern vehicle line-up.
