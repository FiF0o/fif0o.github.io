**Belgacom - iPad sales application:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Designed and built an iPad application for Belgacom to streamline how employees assist customers in purchasing mobile, internet, and bundled services.

{% image "/assets/img/belgacom.jpg", "Mobile application on tablet", "100vw", "Belgacom iPad sales application", "square", "lazy", "auto", "async", "2400", "1600" %}

---

## Problem

Customer-facing teams lacked an integrated tool to effectively guide users through product selection and sales.

Existing systems were disjointed, resulting in inefficiencies and a subpar customer experience at retail and support touchpoints.

---

## Solution

Created a unified web-based interface that enabled employees to browse, compare, and sell telecom products in real-time.

Collaborated on UX research, interface design, and prototyping, ensuring the tool was intuitive, responsive, and aligned with Belgacom’s brand.

The solution empowered teams to better serve customers and increased conversion at point-of-sale.
