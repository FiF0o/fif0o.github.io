**Hartford – Post-Accident Assistance App:**

[[toc]]


## Summary

Developed a mobile application for Hartford Insurance to guide customers through the critical steps following a car accident, providing easy access to relevant information and support services.

---

## Problem

After an accident, customers often feel overwhelmed and unsure of what to do next.

Hartford needed a simple, reliable mobile tool to help policyholders collect key information, understand next steps, and initiate the claims process without confusion or delays.

---

## Solution

Created a user-friendly app with a step-by-step post-accident guide, including emergency contact access, photo/document upload, geolocation tagging, and quick-start claims features.

Designed for clarity and calm under stress, the app helped reduce friction in the claims process and strengthened Hartford’s customer experience in critical moments.
