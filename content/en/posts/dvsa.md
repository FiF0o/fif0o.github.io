**Digital Transformation of Transport:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

As Technical Lead, I led the digital overhaul of the DVSA’s commercial vehicle testing services, replacing disparate legacy systems with a unified, cloud-native platform.

This supported operational efficiency across heavy goods vehicle (HGV) and public service vehicle (PSV) testing nationwide, aligning with the agency’s goal of keeping Britain’s roads moving safely.

## Problem

The DVSA managed vehicle testing—MOTs, commercial HGV/PSV, and enforcement checks—through fragmented, outdated systems. These systems caused administrative delays and inconsistencies in test scheduling and data reporting. 

Meanwhile, over 1.6 million practical driving tests were administered annually, with a ~43% pass rate, and commercial vehicle testing volumes were substantial yet lacked real-time visibility 

---

## Solution

I architected and delivered a cloud-first, service-oriented platform that consolidated test bookings, results management, and reporting across all vehicle categories.
The solution included:

- A centralized scheduling engine for HGV and PSV tests with automated result capture.

- Integration with DVSA’s open-data systems to provide near real-time test volumes and compliance metrics.

- Self-service portals for examiners and private centres to manage book‑ins, cancellations, and test outcomes.

- Embedded analytics dashboards for monitoring test volumes, pass rates, and examiner utilization.

## Outcome

Streamlined test administration processes, significantly reducing duplicate work and data errors.

Enabled real‑time reporting of commercial vehicle and MOT test volumes, enhancing transparency and decision-making.

Improved service efficiency across over 400 test centres, aligning with the DVSA’s vision to support safe, compliant roads.
