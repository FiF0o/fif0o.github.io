**BP – Mobile App for In-Store Deals at Service Stations:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Designed and developed a mobile app for BP customers to discover in-store product promotions at nearby service stations, enhancing the retail experience and boosting engagement beyond fuel purchases.

---

## Problem

BP wanted to increase visibility and uptake of in-store promotions (e.g., food, drinks, convenience items) but lacked a direct, user-friendly digital channel to reach on-the-go customers.

---

## Solution

Built a geolocation-enabled mobile app using Ionic, allowing users to quickly find nearby BP service stations and view current in-store deals.

Designed an intuitive interface focused on speed, relevance, and usability.

The app helped BP promote its retail offering, increased foot traffic, and supported upsell opportunities at the point of sale.
