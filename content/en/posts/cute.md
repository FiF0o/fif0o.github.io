**CuteCircuit – iPad App for Garment Customization:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Designed an iPad application for fashion-tech brand CuteCircuit, enabling users to customize interactive garments and preview them in real time before purchase or production.

---

## Problem

CuteCircuit needed a user-friendly digital tool to showcase the capabilities of its tech-integrated clothing line and let customers experiment with customizable features such as color, pattern, and embedded lighting effects — all without relying on physical samples.

---

## Solution

Developed an intuitive iPad app that allowed users to design garments virtually, previewing their customizations through high-fidelity visuals and interactive controls.

The app provided a seamless experience bridging digital design and wearable tech, enhancing engagement at fashion shows, retail pop-ups, and online campaigns.
