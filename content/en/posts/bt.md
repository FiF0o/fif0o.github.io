**BT – Mobile Video Streaming Platform:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Worked on the design and development of a mobile application for BT, enabling users to access live TV streaming and catch-up video content on demand, directly from their devices.

---

## Problem

BT wanted to expand its digital media offering by delivering live and on-demand video content to mobile users.

The challenge was to create a seamless viewing experience that balanced performance, usability, and content discovery.

---

## Solution

Collaborated with cross-functional teams to build a mobile-first video platform supporting live streams and catch-up TV.

Designed intuitive navigation and playback features, optimized for varied screen sizes and network conditions.

Integrated video APIs and analytics to track engagement and playback quality.

The result was a responsive, engaging app that extended BT’s media reach and improved user retention.
