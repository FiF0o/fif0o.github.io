**RBS – 6 Nations Tournament Mobile App:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Designed and delivered a mobile application for the RBS 6 Nations, providing fans with real-time access to match results, team stats, live commentary, and tournament updates—all from their smartphones.

---

## Problem

RBS, as the title sponsor of the 6 Nations tournament, needed a modern mobile platform to enhance fan engagement and deliver a unified experience across match days. 

Existing digital touchpoints were fragmented, and fans lacked a dedicated, real-time companion app for the tournament.

---

## Solution

Developed a cross-platform mobile app offering live scores, match schedules, team line-ups, and social media integration.

Focused on clean UI, responsive interactions, and performance across varying network conditions.

The app became a go-to companion for fans during the tournament, increasing digital engagement and strengthening the sponsor’s brand presence.
