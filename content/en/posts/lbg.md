**Lloyds Banking Group – Savings Web App Modernization:**

[[toc]]

## Summary

Led the modernization of Lloyds’ customer-facing savings web application to support national digital growth.

As Senior Front‑End Lead, my role encompassed rearchitecting key workflows, optimizing user journeys, and improving performance across desktop and mobile channels.

## Problem

Lloyds experienced rapid growth in digital savings demand, with 3.9 million new savings accounts opened in 2024 and over 20 million mobile banking users logging in nearly 6 billion times per year.

However, the underlying legacy code struggled to scale, resulting in slow build times, performance bottlenecks during peak usage, and suboptimal customer journeys for account openings and product upsell.


---

## Solution

We implemented a full front-end replatforming using modern JavaScript frameworks, containerized CI/CD pipelines, and automated deployment via Jenkins and Docker. Key outcomes included:

- 400% reduction in build times, enabling more frequent feature releases.

- Improved performance during peak periods, handling traffic surges tied to product campaigns and market events.

- Enhanced conversational user flows for account opening and product selection, leading to measurable uplifts in conversion and customer satisfaction.

## Outcome

The platform supported the opening of nearly 4 million savings accounts in 2024, with faster delivery cycles and scalable infrastructure.

Mobile and web experiences were more responsive, reducing friction in onboarding and unlocking opportunities for cross-sell in the savings and ISA product suite.
